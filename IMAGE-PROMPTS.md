# Image generation prompts for chefmelissa.shop

This file gives you a copy-paste prompt for every image on the site, plus exactly where to save the result and what filename to use.

## How to use this file

1. Open ChatGPT (with image generation), DALL-E, Midjourney, or any AI image tool.
2. **First, paste the "STYLE PREFIX"** below into your prompt every time. This makes all 16 images look like they belong on the same site.
3. Then paste the **specific recipe prompt** for that image.
4. Generate at **4:3 aspect ratio** (sometimes called "landscape" or "1024x768"). If your tool only does square, that's fine but expect to crop later.
5. Save the file as a `.jpg` with the **exact filename** listed.
6. Drop the file into `site/assets/img/` on your hosting account, replacing the SVG placeholder of the same name.

## STYLE PREFIX (paste before every recipe prompt)

```
Professional editorial food photography, top-down or 3/4 angle view,
natural daylight from the left, on a rustic light-wood cutting board
or weathered wooden table, neutral linen napkin partially in frame,
photorealistic, high detail, warm and inviting, cookbook style,
shallow depth of field, no text, no labels, no logos, no people,
no hands, 4:3 aspect ratio.
```

## Quality tips

**Best practices for these prompts:**

- Generate at the **highest quality your tool supports** (DALL-E 3 "HD", ChatGPT "Plus" image quality, Midjourney V6+).
- Prefer **landscape (4:3) over square**. The cards on the site are 4:3 and square images will get cropped awkwardly.
- If a generated image looks wrong (text artifacts, weird hand-shaped shadow, alien geometry on the food), **regenerate**. The cost of one extra generation is much less than the cost of an off-looking food photo.
- Save as **.jpg** at 80-90% quality. The cards display at about 400px wide, so generation at 1024x768 is plenty.

**What to avoid:**

- Don't accept images with **visible text or labels** &mdash; those are AI artifacts and Google flags them.
- Don't accept images with **partially-rendered hands or fingers**. Either no hands at all, or a clean cropped wrist.
- Don't accept **glossy stock-photo lighting** with strong overhead spotlight. The "natural daylight from the left" instruction in the style prefix is what creates the editorial look.
