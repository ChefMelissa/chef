Manage your privacy choices

Three things you can do here. None of them require an account.

1. Reopen the cookie consent message

If you're in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, an ad-consent message should appear the first time you visit. To change your choice, click the button below; it will clear the saved decision and prompt you again on your next page load.

2. Turn off Google ad personalisation

Use Google's own controls to stop personalised ads across all sites that show Google ads:

  • Google Ads Settings
  • aboutads.info/choices (US opt-outs)
  • youronlinechoices.eu (EU opt-outs)

Those controls are managed by Google and the advertising industry, not by me. They affect how ads behave across many sites, including this one.

3. Clear what this site stored in your browser

The pantry checklist and the recipe scaler save the data you typed to your own browser. The button below clears just the items this site uses. It does not affect Google's cookies or your browser-wide settings.

Other ways

You can always block cookies in your browser settings, use private/incognito mode, or install a browser extension. The recipes will still read fine.

If you'd like to ask what data I personally hold about you (essentially: any email you sent me), email hello@chefmelissa.shop.
