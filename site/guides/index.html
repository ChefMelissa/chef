Why guides, not just recipes

The recipes on this site are specific dinners I cook. The guides are the patterns I see across all of them — the stuff that makes one recipe link to the next, and that turns "following a recipe" into "actually cooking."

If you have time for one, read the acid-fat-salt-heat guide. It changed how I taste food more than any cookbook ever did.
